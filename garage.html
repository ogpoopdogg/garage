Oil check schedule

How often drivers are asked to check the oil in each vehicle.

A check counts for the vehicle whoever does it, and the next one is counted from it. If nobody checks a vehicle on one of its days, the next driver who takes it is asked. Shop and "Not driving today" shifts are never asked. Changes save right away.
